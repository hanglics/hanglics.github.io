Teaching & mentoring

Sharing what I learn.

Helping students connect the foundations of computer science with the systems they can build.

At the University of Queensland, I teach and give guest lectures in postgraduate computer science, spanning information retrieval, concurrent programming, and web information systems.

My teaching has included practical sessions, consultations, assessment design, and leading the teaching assistant team for INFS7202. I have also mentored students in artificial intelligence and information retrieval as a tutor in UQ residential halls.
